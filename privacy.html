Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Calypso Health (“Calypso,” “we,” “us”) is a thyroid-aware wellness companion. This policy explains what we collect on our website and how we use it. This is a draft for early waitlist use and should be reviewed by counsel before you rely on it legally.

What we collect

  • Waitlist email — if you join the waitlist
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type or IP address that our hosting provider may log automatically

We do not collect lab results, mood logs, or HealthKit data through this website.

How we use your email

  • To notify you about TestFlight / early access
  • To send occasional product updates related to Calypso

We will not sell your email address. You can unsubscribe anytime by emailing us.

Where data is stored

Waitlist emails are stored in our Supabase project. Hosting may be provided by Vercel or a similar provider.

Your choices

  • Ask us to delete your waitlist email
  • Unsubscribe from marketing or launch emails
